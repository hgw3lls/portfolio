Fiction as operational structure for image / sound / code / space.

Tony Yanick is a transdisciplinary artist-educator whose work treats media systems as environments: infrastructural, relational, temporal, participatory, and materially unstable.

Open

Works as environments to enter, activate, and never fully resolve.

My work operates through fiction—not as representation, but as a force that produces conditions.

I am interested in how narrative, myth, mediated images, and technological systems organize collective desire, and how these structures persist, circulate, and mutate across media environments.

The work develops across film, sound, installation, performance, and computational media—not as movement between disciplines, but as interference between forms. Image, sound, code, and spatial systems interact to produce unstable relationships between perception, atmosphere, and interpretation.

I return repeatedly to temporal displacement. Fragments of possible worlds appear out of sequence—past events reconfigured as future projections, speculative structures embedded within existing archives, signals that arrive too early or too late.

Computational systems extend this condition. I engage generative and networked media not simply as tools for production, but as infrastructures that compress, redistribute, and reorganize perception.

There is no stable image at the end of this process—only signals that continue to circulate.
